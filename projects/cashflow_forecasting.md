## Objective

Give finance stakeholders a forward-looking view of cashflow with quantified uncertainty, replacing spreadsheet extrapolation.

## Approach

- Consolidated historical cashflow, invoice, and contract data; engineered seasonality, lag, and calendar features.
- Compared statistical and ML forecasters using rolling-origin backtests and error metrics (MAPE, RMSE).
- Delivered forecasts with optimistic / base / pessimistic scenarios and a repeatable retraining approach.
